Scan with rear phone camera

TraceAPL requests the back camera for QR codes or common barcodes. If scanning is blocked, the page will show a diagnostic message and you can use the manual entry field below.

Tip: for QR codes, fill much of the camera view. For linear barcodes, hold the phone farther back so the full barcode is sharp and horizontal. Avoid glare. Phone camera scanning usually requires HTTPS.

Mobile compatibility notes

  • iOS and Android should work best over HTTPS.
  • TraceAPL requests the rear-facing camera only.
  • If the camera does not start, check the status/debug message above, confirm browser camera permission, and reload the page.
  • Manual entry works even if camera scanning is blocked.
